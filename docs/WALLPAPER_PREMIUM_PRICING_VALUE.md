# Wallpaper Premium — Pricing & Value Analysis

## Current offer (landing page)

- **Price:** ₹99 one-time (displayed as “was ₹299”, Save 67%).
- **Product:** “Premium” unlock for the **wallpaper app** (not the messaging subscription).

---

## What Premium includes (value delivered)

| Feature | User value | Comparable market (India / global) |
|--------|------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Dynamic Island Live Activities** | Year progress, days left, day status on iPhone 14 Pro+ without opening the app | Niche; similar apps often ₹250–600 one-time or subscription |
| **Lock Screen Live Activities + widgets** | Same info on Lock Screen; always visible | Widget apps often ₹80–400 one-time |
| **Full HD wallpaper library** | Thousands of wallpapers, multiple categories (Nature, Minimalist, Anime, People, General) | Free with ads, or ₹250–800 one-time for premium wallpaper apps |
| **Year progress in-app** | Calendar view, progress tracking, motivation | Productivity apps often ₹80–250 one-time |
| **One-tap set wallpaper** | Home screen, lock screen, or both | Part of any good wallpaper app |
| **Weekly new wallpapers** | Ongoing value, no extra cost | Usually subscription or repeated IAP |
| **Pet / engagement (if gated)** | Fun, retention, differentiation | Hard to compare; adds perceived value |

**Conclusion:** The bundle easily justifies **₹150–300** one-time in value. Many competitors charge more for less (e.g. Dynamic Island alone or wallpaper-only premium).

---

## Is ₹99 a good price?

### When ₹99 makes sense

- **Acquisition & reviews:** Low friction, high conversion, more installs and ratings.
- **Launch / positioning:** “Launch price” or “intro offer” to build user base and word-of-mouth.
- **Trust:** First-time buyers more likely to try; you can upsell messaging subscription (₹199/mo) later.

### Downsides of ₹99

- **Underpriced vs value:** You’re giving a lot (Dynamic Island + Live Activities + full library + widgets) for very little. Comparable value elsewhere is often ₹200–500+.
- **Perception:** Some users may assume “cheap = low quality” unless quality is clearly communicated.
- **Raising later:** Moving to ₹149 or ₹199 can cause “you increased price” feedback unless it’s clearly framed as “launch offer ended.”

---

## Recommendation

### Option A — Keep ₹99 (growth focus)

- **Best for:** Maximizing downloads, reviews, and awareness.
- **Do this:**  
  - Frame as **“Launch price”** or **“Intro price”** so you can later move to ₹99–149 without breaking trust.  
  - On the landing page, add a short **“What you get”** or **“One-time, no subscription”** line so the value is explicit and the price feels fair.

### Option B — Move to ₹99–149 (value-based)

- **Best for:** Better revenue per user while still being very accessible.
- **Rationale:** Still “less than a coffee” / impulse range, but closer to the value you deliver.  
- **Copy:** e.g. “Premium — ₹99 one-time” and “Was ₹299” or “Save 65%”.

### Option C — ₹199–299 (premium positioning)

- **Best for:** Positioning as a premium, design/productivity app.
- **Rationale:** Your feature set (especially Dynamic Island + Live Activities) is rare and justifies a higher one-time price.  
- **Risk:** Lower conversion unless you have strong social proof and positioning.

---

## Suggested landing-page tweaks (for any price)

1. **Value clarity:** Add a line like: “One-time purchase. No subscription. Free updates included.” so the offer is clearly “pay once, keep forever.”
2. **What’s included:** A small bullet list next to the CTA (Dynamic Island, Lock Screen, full library, year progress) so the amount of value is obvious.
3. **If you keep ₹99:** Use “Launch price” or “Intro offer” so a future increase to ₹99–149 feels consistent.
4. **If you raise to ₹99–149:** Keep “Was ₹299” or “Save 50%+” to preserve a sense of deal.
