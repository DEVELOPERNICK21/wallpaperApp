## Full Description

### Transform Your iPhone with Beautiful Wallpapers & Year Progress

**Wallpaper** is the ultimate app for iPhone users who want stunning wallpapers and meaningful time tracking—all in one place.

#### 🎨 Beautiful Wallpapers
Browse thousands of high-quality wallpapers across multiple categories:
- **Nature** — Breathtaking landscapes and natural beauty
- **Minimalist** — Clean, elegant designs
- **Anime** — Vibrant animated artwork
- **People** — Stunning portraits
- **General** — Curated collection for every taste

Download and apply wallpapers instantly. Set them as your home screen, lock screen, or both with a single tap.

#### 📅 Year Progress Tracking (Premium)
Track your year's progress right on your Dynamic Island and Lock Screen:
- **Year Progress** — See how much of 2026 you've completed (e.g., "2026 · 63%")
- **Days Left** — Countdown showing days remaining and days passed
- **Day Status** — Track today's progress with percentage done and left

Watch your year unfold in real-time with Live Activities that update automatically. Perfect for staying motivated and aware of time passing.

#### 🏝️ Dynamic Island Features (iPhone 14 Pro+)
Exclusive Dynamic Island integration:
- Live year progress updates
- Days left countdown
- Today's progress tracking
- Beautiful color-coded displays (red for passed, green for remaining, amber for percentage)

- **HD Quality** — High-resolution wallpapers optimized for all iPhone models

- **Easy Download** — Save wallpapers directly to your gallery

- **Year Calculator** — Track your progress with beautiful visualizations
- **Lock Screen Widgets** — Year progress widgets for your Lock Screen
