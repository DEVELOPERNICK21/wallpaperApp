## 🎯 Pricing Tiers

**Price**: ₹0/month
**Target**: Casual users, trial users, privacy-conscious individuals on a budget

**Psychology Applied**:
- **Loss Aversion**: "Forever Free" creates fear of missing out on a permanent offer
- **Habit Formation**: Get users hooked with core features, then upsell
- **Social Proof**: Large free user base builds credibility

**Features**:
- Core E2EE messaging
- Limited chats (5 active)
- 30-day history
- Standard wallpapers
- Basic privacy controls

**Limitations** (shown clearly):
- Only 5 active chats
- 30-day message retention
- Standard wallpapers only

**Conversion Goal**: 15-20% upgrade to Premium within 30 days

## 🧠 Psychology Techniques Used

### 1. **Anchoring Effect**
- Show original prices (₹499, ₹999) crossed out
- Makes current prices feel like massive discounts
- Creates perception of value

### 2. **Scarcity & Urgency**
- "Launch Special" banner
- "Limited time only" messaging
- Creates fear of missing out (FOMO)

### 3. **Social Proof**
- "Recommended" badge on Premium
- User count display
- Trust indicators (7-day refund, cancel anytime)

### 4. **Loss Aversion**
- "Save ₹3,600/year" emphasizes what they'd lose
- "Forever Free" for Basic tier
- Money-back guarantee reduces risk

### 5. **Price Positioning**
- ₹199 (not ₹200) - psychological pricing
- Premium at ₹199 is below ₹200 threshold
- Pro at ₹499 is below ₹500 threshold

### 6. **Decoy Effect**
- Pro tier makes Premium look like better value
- Most users will choose Premium (optimal for revenue)

### 7. **Value Stacking**
- Clear feature progression
- "Everything in X + more" messaging
- Makes upgrades feel incremental

### 8. **Risk Reversal**
- 7-day money-back guarantee
- Cancel anytime
- No long-term contracts
- Reduces purchase anxiety

## 💡 Pricing Rationale

### Why ₹199 for Premium?
1. **Below ₹200 threshold** - psychological barrier
2. **60% discount** - significant savings perception
3. **Competitive** - Lower than Telegram Premium (₹469)
4. **Value-based** - Unique disguise features justify premium
5. **Sustainable** - Covers costs + healthy margin

### Why ₹499 for Pro?
1. **2.5x Premium** - Clear value tier separation
2. **Below ₹500 threshold** - psychological pricing
3. **Enterprise positioning** - Premium feel without being too expensive
4. **50% discount** - Still feels like a deal

### Why Free Tier?
1. **User acquisition** - Low barrier to entry
2. **Viral growth** - Free users invite others
3. **Habit formation** - Get users dependent on app
4. **Upsell opportunity** - Convert to paid
